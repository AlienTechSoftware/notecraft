NoteCraft for macOS

Install the app
1. Drag NoteCraft.app to Applications.
2. Open NoteCraft from Applications.
3. On the first launch after installation, NoteCraft opens a short setup: a welcome page, the local
logging level, your page format, and whether to add the Welcome notebook in that format. Choosing Yes
creates `welcome-to-notecraft` in your notebooks folder when you press Finish, then opens it. Changes
in an existing Welcome notebook will be lost. Other notebooks are unchanged. Choosing No creates
nothing. The setup does not appear again unless you reset the preferences.

The Welcome notebook is an ordinary user-owned folder. You can edit or delete it like any other
notebook. The app bundle includes the source used to create it.
