This page is part of the OCR demo. Status: **MVP** for the chart; sketches are a later stage.

A bar chart keeps its numbers in the picture. NoteCraft reads the words it can read, such as the title and the labels. The bars are not read as numbers yet.

![A bar chart titled Notes created per month, with bars for September (12), October (25) and November (31)](assets/ocr-chart.png)

A hand-drawn sketch is harder. Its words may be read, but its boxes and arrows are not understood yet.

![A rough whiteboard sketch: an Inbox box with an arrow to an Archive box, and a Review oval with a line to the word Weekly](assets/ocr-sketch.png)
