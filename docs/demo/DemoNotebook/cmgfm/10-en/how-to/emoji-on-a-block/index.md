Every block has a small **+** at its top left. Click it to open the emoji grid. Choose an emoji
and it sits beside the **+**. To take an emoji off, click it on the block.

## Try it

1. Click the **+** at the top left of this block.
2. Scroll the grid and choose an emoji. It appears beside the **+**.
3. Click that emoji on the block. It goes.
