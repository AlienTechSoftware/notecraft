Right-click offers what you can do next, and the choices depend on where you click. Keyboard shortcuts move you between pages without the mouse.

Where you right-clickWhat the menu offers
A page in the navigatorNew page, Add a sub-page, Lock read only or Unlock, Rename, Move or Copy, Copy link to page, Open folder, Page versions, Delete
The page's textPaste, Cut and Copy, the insertions (table, link, equation, date and time, picture, file), and Lock read only or Unlock. A locked page offers only Unlock
Empty space in the page listNew page, and Lock read only or Unlock for the open page
Empty space in the navigatorNew notebook, Open a folder, Show the notebooks folder, and Lock read only or Unlock for the open page

Lock a page

Lock read only keeps the lock inside the page, so it goes with the page when you copy or sync its folder. A locked page keeps its title and its words: the title box does not take typing, and an edit is refused. Choose Unlock from the same menu to edit it again.

Moving around

  • Ctrl+Page Up and Ctrl+Page Down: the page above or below in the page list.
  • Alt+Left and Alt+Right, or the mouse's back and forward buttons: back and forward through the pages you have opened.
  • Search all pages: the box at the top right finds words in every page. Enter opens the chosen result.
