Themes

The sun or moon at the top right opens the theme chooser: light and dark, each in seven accents, 14 in all. A swatch picks that mode. The switch under the swatches makes NoteCraft follow your system's light or dark setting again.

Every colour in every theme is checked against its background by a test, so no theme can show you text you cannot read.

Colours for notebooks and sections

Right-click a notebook, section group or section, then Colour. Nine colours, shown on the icon and on the edge of the row. The groups in this notebook each have one.

The page's own view

Button beside the pageWhat it does
< >Opens the page's files in the code view
MagnifierSearch all pages
ArrowsFull page: the notebooks step aside until you press it again

View adds rule lines behind the page, zoom, and hiding the page title. Rule lines and zoom are remembered.

Opening where you left off

NoteCraft reopens the page you closed on, with the sections above it expanded.

Settings on disk

All of this is a few lines in a small settings file outside your notebooks, so a notebook never carries one person's window settings into another person's sync folder.
