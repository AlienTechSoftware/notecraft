A tag marks a line as something: a thing to do, something important, a question to answer. Click in a line, then Home and the Tags box. Asking for the same tag again takes it off.

To do

  • ☐ Tick the box above by clicking it

A to-do line is a task list item, which every Markdown reader understands.

The other tags

#important Leave on Friday, not Saturday.

#question Does the train stop at the airport?

#idea Keep the tickets in the page, not in the mail.

#remember The booking reference is in the attached file.

NoteCraft draws the mark instead of the word, but the word is what is in the file, so your notes stay readable in any editor and searchable from a terminal. A word that only looks like a tag, like #ideas without a space after it, is left alone.
